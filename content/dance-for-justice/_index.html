Get Involved

More than a dance — a statement

Our house parties are more than just a celebration of movement — they are a statement for safety and inclusion. Through the vibrant, inclusive energy of fusion dance, we create space where bodies are celebrated, boundaries are respected, and community thrives in safety and solidarity.

We dance in protest of rape culture — a culture that normalizes violence, silences survivors, and devalues consent. We reject the myths, the silence, and the systems that perpetuate sexual harm. In their place, we move with intention, with joy, and with fierce compassion.

All proceeds go directly to organizations working to end sexual violence and support survivors.

In our first edition in 2025, we hosted more than 20 community events across Europe, raising more than 4 000 €.

Together, we embody a future where consent is culture, accountability is the norm, and everyone has the right to move freely without fear.

We are not fighting any individual, but a system. Anyone can join as long as they share those values.

We dance not just for ourselves — but for the world we are building.

How to participate

As a participant

Join one of your local party, and give time, money, or goods to fight sexual violence.

{{}}

As an Organizer

  1. Host an event — a house party or a regular dance gathering in November, around the 25th (International Day for the Elimination of Violence Against Women).
  2. Get listed — add your event to the official spreadsheet.
  3. Get the playlist — a curated playlist is provided in collaboration with fusion DJs.
  4. Create your flyer — use our Canva template.
  5. Join the main date — if you're partying on November 25th, join the European Fusion Discord to see other parties streaming live.
  6. Run a little fundraiser — choose an organization fighting sexual violence (we recommend a local one; see below) and raise funds by:
    • Having a donation box at the door
    • Selling tickets to the party or to a class
    • Selling art, drinks, or food
    • Holding an auction
  7. Share the impact — donate the proceeds and announce your fundraising effort on social media. Have fun!

As a DJ

  • Offer a Spotify playlist through the DJ contribution form.
  • Your playlist will be considered for the official collection shared with all organizers worldwide.

As a Teacher

  • Offer a class to your local organizer that can be used as a fundraiser — entry fees go directly to the chosen charity.
  • It can be part of the event evening or a standalone session before the main date.

Recommended organizations

  • RAINN — The nation's largest anti-sexual violence organization
  • Libre de ses choix | Le planning familial
  • Right To Be
  • No Means No Worldwide

We encourage you to also support your local organization.
